Compare two published consulting scopes

A finance team deciding which invoices to automate needs different evidence from a team ready to test a matching application. These two UK G-Cloud 14 listings show what to ask for at each stage.

Digital Space · readiness assessment

Digital Space describes workshops, questionnaires and data exploration with IT and business teams. The work identifies use cases, assesses data quality and can outline a statement of work for a proof of value.

Published rate £595 to £1,495 per unit per day. Read Digital Space’s scope and pricing.

Suggested acceptance test Ask the finance lead to review the proposed use case, sample-data gaps and pilot plan. Can they decide whether to fund a build?

EY · application development

EY describes discovery, prototyping and application development through alpha and beta. Ask the bidder which phase the proposed fee covers and what software you receive at the end of it.

Published rate £200 to £2,600 per unit per day. Read EY’s scope and pricing.

Suggested acceptance test for a prototype Give the team held-back invoices. Have a clerk replay them and record matches, incorrect matches and referrals before signing off.
